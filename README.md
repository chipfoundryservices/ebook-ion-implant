# Book #8: Ion Implant & Dopant Engineering
## Deep Junction Formation, Damage Annealing, and the Economics of Ion Source Monopoly

**Book #8 in the ChipFoundryServices Technical Series**

Ion implantation remains one of the highest-ROIC equipment segments in semiconductor manufacturing. This book covers the physics of ion acceleration, dopant profiles, lattice damage, and thermal annealing—and explains why ion implanter suppliers maintain 40-50% gross margins through extended equipment lifespans and service monopolies.
