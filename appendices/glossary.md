# Appendix A: Ion Implantation Glossary

- **Amorphous Silicon ($a\text{-Si}$):** Silicon devoid of long-range crystalline order, created when implant damage exceeds the critical threshold.
- **Beam Energy Purity:** Absence of unwanted kinetic energy components caused by charge-exchange collisions along the beamline.
- **Channeling:** Deep penetration of ions guided down open crystallographic axes without experiencing major nuclear collisions.
- **Critical Dose ($\Phi_{\text{crit}}$):** The minimum ion dose required to convert a crystalline substrate into an amorphous layer.
- **De-Chucking:** Process of neutralizing electrostatic charge between wafer and chuck to allow mechanical release without breakage.
- **End-of-Range (EOR) Defects:** Dislocation loops formed just beyond the original amorphous/crystalline interface after thermal annealing.
- **Freeman Source:** Thermionic arc-discharge ion source utilizing an internal filament and magnetic field for high-current operation.
- **Lindhard-Scharff-Schiøtt (LSS) Theory:** Classical formulation dividing ion energy loss into nuclear stopping and electronic stopping.
- **LSA (Laser Spike Annealing):** Sub-millisecond surface heating using laser scanning to activate dopants with zero diffusion.
- **Mass Resolution ($m / \Delta m$):** Metric of a magnetic analyzer's ability to separate neighboring isotopic masses.
- **Pearson IV Distribution:** Four-parameter mathematical profile model accounting for skewness and kurtosis in real implant distributions.
- **PLAD (Plasma Doping):** Plasma immersion technique applying pulsed negative voltage to implant vertical 3D structures conformally.
- **Projected Range ($R_p$):** Mean depth of the implanted dopant concentration peak below the target surface.
- **Solid-Phase Epitaxial Regrowth (SPER):** Thermally driven recrystallization of amorphous silicon using the underlying lattice as a template.
- **Straggle ($\Delta R_p$):** Standard deviation of the projected ion range along the incident beam direction.
- **Transient Enhanced Diffusion (TED):** Anomalous, orders-of-magnitude surge in dopant diffusion driven by interstitial clusters during initial heating.
